Pattern: Side-by-side stacked bars with smart label placement

One row = one category. Each row has two bars (A baseline / B current). Each bar stacks 3 segments. Small segments use leader lines to outside labels.

What this demonstrates:
  • Margin convention (translate group into plot area)
  • Two stacked bars per x-tick (hard for Chart.js)
  • Semi-transparent fill + solid stroke for segment definition
  • Inside label when segment ≥ 35px, leader line outside otherwise
  • Greedy collision avoidance for stacked leader labels
